Experiment 003 — galplane-drift-sidelobe-stress
Companion notes to 003_galplane-drift-sidelobe-stress.pdf
Date: 2026-07-23


SETUP
-----
Galactic-plane sidelobe stress test. Identical telescope, beams, noise
seeds, and map-making recipe as experiments 001/002 — the only change is
the LST window: start 2024-04-16 03:34 UTC (LST ≈ 280°), so the same
az = 0°, el = 52°/50°/48° drift sweeps the Dec ≈ +9° strip across the
galactic plane, b = +7° → −6°, crossing at l ≈ 43° (inner galaxy). The
beam-averaged TOD peaks at ~290 K vs ~45 K baseline; the airy − gauss
TOD difference reaches ±5 K as the sidelobe rings lead/trail the main
lobe over the plane. Sky: GDSM rotated to equatorial (this experiment
was the one that exposed the galactic-frame bug in
`limTOD.GDSM_sky_model` — its first run showed no plane crossing at all;
see 001's FRAME CORRECTION note and the repo CHANGELOG).

Scenarios as in 001/002: matched gauss, matched airy, and mismatch
(tapered-aperture sky through the Gaussian operator = unmodelled
sidelobes, sharing the gauss operator, mask, prior, and noise).


RESULTS
-------
Residual RMS vs truth (K, note — not mK):
  gauss    / no HP       23.1 K
  gauss    / HP 2 mHz    31.4 K
  airy     / no HP       25.3 K
  airy     / HP 2 mHz    32.2 K
  mismatch / no HP       23.9 K
  mismatch / HP 2 mHz    30.7 K

Unmodelled-sidelobe map error (mismatch − gauss, same mask & noise):
  no HP       RMS 8.0 K    max |Δ| 23.9 K
  HP 2 mHz    RMS 4.5 K    max |Δ| 13.3 K

1. THE SIDELOBE SYSTEMATIC EXPLODES ON THE PLANE: from 0.09–0.23 K RMS
   at b ≈ +50° (experiment 002) to 4.5–8.0 K RMS here — a factor of
   ~35–50, with peak map errors of 13–24 K. Unmodelled −23 dB sidelobes
   go from subdominant to a leading-order error when the rings sweep
   through inner-plane emission. Any drift-scan survey whose strip
   crosses (or passes near) the plane needs the true beam in the
   operator — or must mask/excise the crossing.
2. THE HIGH-PASS FILTER NOW HURTS, badly (23 → 31 K for gauss): the
   plane crossing IS a large low-frequency signal, and the 2 mHz filter
   removes tens-of-K of genuine sky. The HP-helps conclusion from 001
   is patch-dependent — filtering strategy must adapt to sky brightness
   along the drift (e.g. filter only off-plane segments).
3. Overall residuals (23–32 K on a ~100–300 K sky) are poor across ALL
   scenarios, including matched beams: fractional 1/f + white noise
   scale with the bright sky, the prior σ = std(truth) is enormous
   (~70 K) and so constrains little, and 3 beam-center Decs cannot
   deconvolve the huge dynamic range. Drift-scanning across the inner
   plane is hard even before beam systematics.


CAVEATS
-------
- The mask differs slightly from 001/002 (321/276 pixels vs 317/277) —
  same design, different LST window. Cross-experiment comparisons of
  RMS-to-truth are indicative only; the mismatch − gauss map-error
  metric is the robust cross-experiment quantity (same-mask, same-noise
  by construction within each experiment).
- The pixel-RMS is dominated by the brightest plane pixels; a per-|b| or
  masked-plane breakdown would separate "plane pixels are hopeless" from
  "plane leakage contaminates the off-plane strip". Not yet done — worth
  doing before quoting these numbers.
- Same generic caveats as 001/002: fractional noise model, strong
  smoothed-truth prior, single channel, single realization, symmetric
  achromatic-shape beams, no RFI/atmosphere/ground.
